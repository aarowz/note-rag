# Supplier risk primer

Lightweight Markdown-only corpus for local RAG experiments.

## Single sourcing

**Single sourcing** concentrates volume with one supplier for cost or quality reasons. It increases exposure to disruption compared to qualified dual sourcing.

## Dual sourcing

**Dual sourcing** maintains two qualified suppliers for critical items. It often raises coordination overhead but improves resilience when one lane slips.

## Scorecards

Supplier **scorecards** summarize delivery reliability, quality incidents, and responsiveness. They work best when metrics align with what buyers actually optimize.
