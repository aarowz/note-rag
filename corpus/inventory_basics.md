# Inventory basics

Short notes on vocabulary useful for supply-chain style documentation retrieval demos.

## SKU and identifiers

A **SKU** (stock keeping unit) is the smallest unit you track for fulfillment or planning. Multiple SKUs may roll up into a **product family** for reporting.

## Safety stock

**Safety stock** is extra inventory held to buffer demand variability and supply uncertainty. It is not “free”: it ties up working capital and storage capacity.

## Lead time

**Lead time** is the elapsed time from placing an order to having usable inventory available to promise or ship. Supplier lead times often dominate replenishment planning.

## Bills of materials

A **bill of materials (BOM)** lists components required to build a finished good. Engineering BOMs and manufacturing BOMs may differ in granularity.
